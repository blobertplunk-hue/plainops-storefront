Lesson 14: Getting in Order

Choose a partner. Work with a partner from the start. Choose your partner. Work together until the independent Show What You Know.

Warm-Up

Notice and Wonder about three unlabeled number lines with flippers that allow a ball to travel to a star. Share what you notice and wonder.

Activity 1

Put Them in Order. Work with your partner to order fractions from least to greatest using a strategy that makes sense. Source examples include 1/8, 3/12, 1/2 and 5/6, 11/10, 8/5. Explain how you compared them.

Activity 2

Which Is the Greatest? Use the Lesson 14 fraction-card set. Reveal cards one at a time and place four fractions from least to greatest; once a card is placed, do not move it. Discard a card if it cannot fit. Discuss how you decided where each fraction belongs.

Synthesis

Review the strategies used to compare and order fractions: visual models, benchmark numbers, and equivalent fractions with common numerators or denominators. Explain which strategy worked best for a pair and why.

SWYK

Complete Show What You Know 1.14 independently: compare 3/10 and 2/6; compare 99/100 and 9/10; then order 5/12, 8/6, 4/10, and 7/5 from least to greatest. Show your thinking.
